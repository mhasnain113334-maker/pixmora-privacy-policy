PixMoRa Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PixMoRa. PixMoRa is an AI prompt library application that provides creative text prompts for profile pictures, gaming logos, portraits, anime concepts, and other creative uses. This policy explains how we handle user information.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google, Firebase Authentication may process information such as your name, email address, and account identifier.

Depending on the features you use, the app may also store preferences and favorite prompts locally on your device.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide Google sign-in and account authentication.
  • To provide access to the prompt library.
  • To save favorite prompts and preferences.
  • To maintain and improve app functionality.

4. Photos and Images

PixMoRa provides text prompts. It does not directly generate, upload, or process users' photos or images. Users may copy prompts and use them with third-party AI services independently.

5. Third-Party Services

PixMoRa uses Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication to support account authentication.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Information

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Google and Firebase to provide authentication services or disclosed when required by law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Authentication information may be retained while an account remains active, subject to applicable service-provider retention practices.

Users may contact us to request account and associated data deletion.

9. Children's Privacy

PixMoRa is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is legally required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
